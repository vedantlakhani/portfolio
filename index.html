01 — The Window

The desk always faces the window.

In Mumbai, his study table faced a window, and the window faced the night sky. He watched one view for fifteen years like a time series: the open ground filled in, a tower went up, the air index climbed. He was taking notes before he knew what they were for.

One window, ~15 years, Mumbai. The view was a dataset before he knew the word. In Durham today the bed sits in the wrong corner of the room — so the desk can face the window.

03 — The Prophecy

November 2024. He wrote down what he intended to exist.

Not a plan — a spec, filed inside a graduate application. He had been circling it for years: at Barclays he had already built the bank’s first travel-emissions carbon tracker. The seeds were old. The night he wrote them down, they got a deadline.

Graduate Application — Statement of PurposeNOV 2024

“In the long term, I plan to launch a startup focused on real-time carbon tracking and offset systems. The platform will monitor emissions and provide users with personalized strategies to reduce their carbon footprint.”

04 — Dawn · Veridian, Founder

Twelve months later, he founded it.

Veridian shipped in November 2025 — a carbon tracker with 10 Claude API functions that read your day and suggest one change. EPA/DEFRA-anchored math, 14 screens, a 15-section PRD, and a gamification loop — Green Points, streaks, 16 achievements — with WAU-3 as the north star. Real and shipped, not roadmap.

The same invisible thing, third attempt: corporate carbon at Barclays, industrial costs at AxNano, personal footprint here. Carbon has no voice. He gives it one.
