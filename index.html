Veridian
iOS and Android
0 to 1, solo
2025 to present
Consumer. Climate.

Most people have no idea what an ordinary day costs the planet.

You cannot fix what you cannot see, and nobody wants to spend their evening typing in what they ate and how they got to work. That is the real problem, not a missing feature.

I looked at four existing apps, Klima, Joro, Greenly, Miles. Every one of them asks the user to log their own life by hand, so people quit within days.

Veridian watches what you already do, your driving, your food, your energy use, and turns it into one number and one clear next step. No typing. The goal is not a bigger number to feel guilty about. It is making an invisible cost visible enough to actually change it.

Real screens from my own use. Not seed data, not mockups.

Product choices worth explaining
Missing a day
Most trackers punish a missed day by resetting your streak to zero, so people just delete the app. Veridian forgives a missed day instead of wiping out your progress.
How sure is the number
The emissions model shows how confident it is in its own estimate. A number labeled as a guess earns more trust than a fake exact one.
Your phone's battery
Seven days of motion history with no real battery drain, using only what Android allows for background tracking. An app that drains your battery gets deleted no matter how good the model is.
Status
MVP built, code here, public launch targeted later this year. A portfolio piece and an intended company. I am building it like the latter.
